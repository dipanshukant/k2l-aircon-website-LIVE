5Why it happens

Not cold

Most often a dirty filter or coil, or low gas from a small leak. Sometimes a weak capacitor or a faulty sensor stops the compressor from starting. We check the outdoor unit too, not just the indoor one.

Water leaking

Usually a choked drain pipe or a dirty drain tray. Slime builds up and the water has nowhere to go but out the front. A frozen coil from low gas can also drip when it melts.

Noisy

Loose panels rattle. A dirty or cracked blower wheel wobbles. Grinding or loud humming from the outdoor unit can mean a fan motor or compressor problem, worth checking early.

Won't turn on or keeps tripping

Could be the remote, the breaker, a burnt capacitor or the control board. Many units flash an error code or blinking light. Send us a photo, it helps us bring the right parts.

6Check these first

Some problems you can fix yourself in five minutes. Try these before you call anyone.

  1. Remote. Change the batteries. Make sure the mode is Cool (the snowflake), not Fan or Dry.
  2. Temperature. Set 24 to 25°C. Setting 16°C does not cool faster, it only makes the unit work harder.
  3. Filters. Open the front panel and slide them out. Grey and dusty? Rinse with water, let them dry, put them back.
  4. Power. Check that the aircon breaker is on. If it trips again straight away, stop resetting it and call us.
  5. Outdoor unit. Is the fan spinning? Is it blocked by things stored around it?

Still not working? WhatsApp us a short video of what the aircon is doing. We can often tell the problem before we come.
