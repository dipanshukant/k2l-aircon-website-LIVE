1Jobs we do most in Bedok

Old units that keep breaking

Units over ten years old often need the same repair again and again. We tell you honestly when a repair is worth it, and when a second-hand unit from $700 makes more sense.

R22 aircon

Many older units still use R22 gas, which is being phased out and getting expensive. If your R22 unit needs a top-up every few months, it has a leak, and replacing is usually cheaper in the long run.

Homes near the East Coast

Salty sea air can corrode outdoor units faster. At every service we check the outdoor coil, casing and brackets, not just the indoor unit.

Usually yes. WhatsApp us before 12pm with your block and the problem. Bedok is close to our Tampines office.

Do you still top up R22 gas?

Yes. We top up R22, R410A and R32 from $80 a unit, leak check included. If an R22 unit keeps losing gas, we will tell you if replacing costs less over time.
