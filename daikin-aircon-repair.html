2Common Daikin problems

Error code on the remote

Daikin units can show codes like U4 (indoor and outdoor unit not talking to each other) or U0 (low refrigerant). Send us the code and a photo.

One room cold, one room warm

On a multi-split, this usually points to that one indoor unit or its piping, not the whole system.

Water dripping

Most often a choked drain pipe or dirty drain tray. A service or chemical overhaul usually fixes it.

6Questions people ask

Do you repair Daikin aircon?

Yes. Wall units, multi-split systems and cassettes. Repair starts from $40, and we quote before we start.

What does Daikin error U4 mean?

U4 means the indoor and outdoor units are not communicating. It can be wiring, power to the outdoor unit or a control board. Turn the breaker off and on once. If it comes back, call us.
