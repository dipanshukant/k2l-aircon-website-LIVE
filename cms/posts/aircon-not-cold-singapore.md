Aircon not cold is one of the most common problems we get called for in Singapore. The unit is running, the fan is blowing, but the room just will not cool down. The good news is that many causes are simple, and a few you can check yourself in five minutes. Here are the eight causes our technicians see most often, what each one feels like, and what to do about it.

## Start with these quick checks

Before you call anyone, try these. They solve more problems than you might think:

1. **Check the mode.** The remote should show Cool (the snowflake), not Fan, Dry or Auto.
2. **Check the temperature.** Set it to 24 or 25°C. Setting 16°C does not cool faster. It only makes the unit run harder.
3. **Change the remote batteries.** Weak batteries can send the wrong settings.
4. **Close doors and windows.** An open door to the kitchen or corridor can beat even a good unit.

Still warm after 20 minutes? Read on.

## 1. Dirty air filters

This is the number one cause. Filters catch dust before it reaches the coil. When they are clogged, less air passes through, so less cool air reaches the room.

**What to do:** Open the front panel, slide out the filters and look. If they are grey and furry, rinse them with water, let them dry and put them back. If your aircon is not cold after that, the dirt has probably reached the coil too.

## 2. Dirty coil and blower wheel

Behind the filters sits the cooling coil, and below it the blower wheel that pushes air out. In our humid climate, dust sticks to the wet coil and turns into slime. A clogged coil cannot absorb heat properly, and a dirty blower wheel moves less air.

**What to do:** This needs a technician. A regular [aircon servicing](/aircon-servicing) cleans the parts within reach. If the unit has gone a year or more without a deep clean, a chemical overhaul is usually the better fix.

## 3. Low refrigerant gas

Your aircon cools using refrigerant gas. It does not get used up. If the gas is low, there is a leak somewhere. Signs include ice forming on the pipes, a hissing sound, or air that is cool but never properly cold.

**What to do:** Do not just top up and hope. A good technician checks the gas pressure, looks for the leak and repairs it first. Otherwise the gas leaks out again and you pay twice.

## 4. Outdoor unit blocked or dirty

The outdoor unit releases the heat taken out of your room. If its coil is caked in dust, or it is boxed in by stored items, the heat has nowhere to go and cooling drops.

**What to do:** Clear the space around the outdoor unit. If the fins look grey with dust, ask for the outdoor coil to be cleaned during your next service.

## 5. Faulty capacitor or compressor not starting

The capacitor gives the compressor and fan motor the kick they need to start. When it weakens, the outdoor unit may hum but not run, or run only now and then. The indoor fan keeps blowing, so it feels like the aircon is on, but the air is not cold.

**What to do:** Listen at the outdoor unit. If the fan is not spinning while the indoor unit runs, switch off and call for [aircon repair](/aircon-repair). A capacitor is a fairly small fix. Leaving it can damage the compressor, which is not.

## 6. Faulty sensor or control board

A temperature sensor tells the unit when the room is cool enough. If it fails, the aircon may think the room is already cold and stop cooling early. Many units also flash an error code or a blinking light when the control board detects a problem.

**What to do:** Take a photo of any error code or count the blinks, then send it to your technician. It helps them bring the right part on the first visit.

## 7. Unit too small for the room

Sometimes nothing is broken at all. A 9,000 BTU unit in a large living room, or a room that gets strong afternoon sun, may simply not keep up on hot days. As a rough guide, 9,000 BTU suits most bedrooms, 12,000 BTU a master bedroom, and living rooms usually need 18,000 BTU or more.

**What to do:** If your unit runs nonstop on hot afternoons but copes fine at night, it may be undersized. Blinds or curtains help. Replacing it with the right size is the real fix.

## 8. An old unit at the end of its life

Every aircon wears out eventually. Older units also tend to have lower energy ratings, so they cost more to run. You can compare ratings using the [NEA energy label](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/climate-change-energy-efficiency/energy-efficiency/household-sector/the-energy-label), which goes from 1 tick to 5 ticks.

**What to do:** If repairs keep coming back, compare the cost of fixing it against replacing it. A washed and tested [second-hand aircon](/second-hand-aircon) is often cheaper than repeated repairs on a tired unit.

## How to stop it happening again

Most "aircon not cold" calls trace back to dirt. Rinse your filters every two to four weeks, book servicing every three to six months, and keep the outdoor unit clear. HDB's [home care guide for air-conditioners](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/renovation-and-maintenance/home-maintenance/home-care-guide/air-conditioners) has more simple tips for everyday care.
## When to call a technician

Call for help if your aircon is still not cold after cleaning the filters, if you see ice on the pipes, if the outdoor unit is not running, or if the breaker trips. Do not keep resetting a breaker that trips again straight away.
